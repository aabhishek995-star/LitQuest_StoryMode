About this adaptation

This is a linear study adaptation. All 21 numbered chapters are presented in sequence. The novel’s final section is headed “The Girl from the Sea” and contains the closing chapters. Travel through two interactive story stops in every chapter. Make interpretive choices, receive detailed explanations, then continue along the novel’s fixed storyline. Your choices never rewrite the author’s events.
