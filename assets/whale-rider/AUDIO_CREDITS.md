# LitQuest – The Whale Rider Story Mode audio

The prototype includes original, procedurally synthesised audio files generated specifically for this build:
- `ocean-surf.wav` – stylised rolling surf ambience
- `coastal-wind.wav` – soft coastal wind ambience
- `whale-call-original.wav` – stylised tonal whale-call sound design (not a field recording)
- `rainfall.wav` – stylised rain texture
- `distant-thunder.wav` – stylised distant thunder rumble

These are original synthetic effects, not recordings of actual Māori ceremonies, people, or wildlife. The whale-call is deliberately labelled as stylised rather than documentary audio. A later production pass can replace it with a properly licensed scientific recording, with attribution, after listening and selecting an appropriate excerpt.
