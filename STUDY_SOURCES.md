# New Zealand Literature — study content notes and sources

## Course structure

The New Zealand Literature syllabus in this project follows the five-unit list supplied by the student:

- Unit 1: Poetry — Allen Curnow, Hone Tuwhare, Mike Subritzky (two poems), Anne Kennedy and Peter Bland.
- Unit 2: Prose — Miriama Evans.
- Unit 3: Drama — Bruce Mason.
- Unit 4: Fiction — Witi Ihimaera, Keri Hulme and Alan Duff.
- Unit 5: Short Stories — Katherine Mansfield, Witi Ihimaera, Frank Sargeson and Patricia Grace.

## Question bank design

There are 20 original MCQs per prescribed text, with 25 each for *The Whale Rider*, “Politics and Maori Literature” and *The End of the Golden Weather* (15 texts; 315 questions total). Questions test author/form, central focus, themes, motifs, voice, context and interpretation. They are original practice questions, not copied past-paper questions and not predictions of an examination. Students should verify close-reading details against their prescribed editions and lecturer notes. For some works, the available public references support broad context and thematic framing rather than every possible line-level interpretation.

## The Whale Rider Story Mode

Story Mode contains all 21 numbered chapters. The supplied edition places the heading “The Girl from the Sea” before Chapters Twenty and Twenty-one, so the map shows it as a section divider rather than inventing a separate extra chapter. It is designed as a linear study companion: a paraphrased chapter account, character notes, themes, literary craft and an exam focus. No branching choice changes the novel's events or ending. The chapter summaries are not a substitute for reading the novel and do not reproduce its prose. The uploaded scanned edition was used as the primary course source; secondary study references were used only to help map chapter sequence and broad plot points.
